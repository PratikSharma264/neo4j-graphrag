Codesigned Shared Decision-Making Diabetes Management Plan Tool for Adolescents With Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus and Their Parents: Prototype Development and Pilot Test evaluates the process of codesigning a clinic-based shared decision-making diabetes management plan tool alongside a patient and parent advisory board (PAB) consisting of six adolescents with type 1 diabetes mellitus and their parents. The study found six key quality-of-life measurement domains affecting families—stress, fear and worry, mealtime struggles, assumptions and judgments, feeling abnormal, and conflict—and highlighted that adolescents prioritize social inclusion and reducing family conflict over traditional clinical health outcomes. Based on these findings, the paper recommends utilizing shared decision-making tools in clinical encounters that focus directly on diabetes distress, conflict resolution strategies, and establishing mutually verbalized common goals rather than imposing authoritarian behavioral contracts.
