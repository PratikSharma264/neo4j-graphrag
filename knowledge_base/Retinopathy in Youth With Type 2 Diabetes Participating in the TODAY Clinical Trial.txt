“Retinopathy in Youth With Type 2 Diabetes Participating in the TODAY Clinical Trial” evaluates the prevalence of diabetic retinopathy in a cohort of 517 youth (aged 10–17 years at baseline) with type 2 diabetes of 2 to 8 years duration using standardized central grading of fundus photographs. The study found an overall retinopathy prevalence of 13.7%, with all affected participants exhibiting mild or very mild nonproliferative diabetic retinopathy and no cases of macular edema or proliferative disease. Retinopathy risk was significantly higher among participants who were older, had a longer diabetes duration, or had higher mean HbA1c  levels, whereas those in the highest body mass index (BMI) tertile unexpectedly presented with a lower prevalence. Although specific clinical recommendations and guidelines were not provided, the authors note that the natural history and progression of diabetic retinopathy in youth with type 2 diabetes will be further clarified through continued follow-up of this cohort.
