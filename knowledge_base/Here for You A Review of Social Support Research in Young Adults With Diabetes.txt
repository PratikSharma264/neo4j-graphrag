"Here for You: A Review of Social Support Research in Young Adults With Diabetes" evaluates the types and sources of social support received by young adults managing type 1 or type 2 diabetes and their associations with behavioral, psychosocial, and glycemic outcomes, while also discussing intervention research and future directions for care. The paper's findings indicate that while young adults experience a decline in parental support and unique challenges or conflicts in peer and romantic relationships, social support serves as a critical protective factor that buffers stress, reduces psychological distress, and correlates with better treatment adherence, self-management behaviors, and lower A1C levels. The review outlines the clinical application of leveraging various support systems—including family, friends, diabetes communities, and health care providers—to improve psychosocial and clinical outcomes, and highlights recommendations for developing and testing tailored behavioral and social support interventions specifically designed to meet the unique developmental and transitional needs of young adults with diabetes.
