"Disordered Eating Behaviors and Eating Disorders in Youth With Type 2 Diabetes: A Systematic Review" evaluates disordered eating behaviors (DEBs) in youth diagnosed with type 2 diabetes by identifying the most common DEBs, analyzing potential risk and protective factors, and reviewing existing screening tools and interventions utilized for this population. Findings indicate that approximately half of the youth study participants had elevated scores on the Diabetes Eating Problem Survey–Revised, showing a higher likelihood of engaging in DEBs compared to youth with type 1 diabetes, with key risk factors including obesity, body weight/shape concerns, and caregiver subclinical binge eating. Recommendations highlight a critical need for further research to construct a framework for understanding influential variables, to advance screening tools, and to design effective targeted interventions.
