The article Psychological interventions for adherence, metabolic control, and coping with stress in adolescents with type 1 diabetes: a systematic review evaluates the variations in effectiveness among psychological interventions—specifically cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), coping skills training (CST), stress management, and psychotherapy—on adherence, metabolic control, and coping with stress in adolescents with type 1 diabetes based on a review of 24 studies published between 1990 and 2019. The findings indicate that while behavioral interventions utilizing CBT principles have a superior positive impact on regimen adherence, metabolic control improves significantly through CST and certain CBT approaches, and stress management and psychotherapy show strong promise in helping adolescents manage daily diabetes-related stressors. Consequently, the review suggests that psychological interventions hold promise for enhancing diabetes management in youth, though additional research is required to better understand their specific effects.
