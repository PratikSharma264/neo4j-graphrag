"Adaptation of an Adult Web Application for Type 1 Diabetes Self-management to Youth Using the Behavior Change Wheel to Tailor the Needs of Health Care Transition: Qualitative Interview Study" evaluates the needs and factors influencing diabetes self-management among youth aged 14 to 24 living with type 1 diabetes to guide the adaptation of an adult self-guided web application (Support). The findings categorize self-management factors into internal and external barriers and facilitators, highlighting that youth desire reliable, practical, and novel educational content, alongside supervised peer exchange and simplified information search processes, to navigate the challenges of transitioning to adult care.
