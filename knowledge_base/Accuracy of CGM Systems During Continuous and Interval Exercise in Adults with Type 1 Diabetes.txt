The study Accuracy of CGM Systems During Continuous and Interval Exercise in Adults with Type 1 Diabetes evaluates the accuracy of several continuous glucose monitoring systems available in Italy (Dexcom G5, Dexcom G6, Eversense, Medtronic Guardian, and Enlite) in 22 adults with type 1 diabetes during moderate continuous exercise and interval exercise sessions. The findings indicate that physical exercise negatively impacts sensor accuracy, causing sensors to consistently overestimate blood glucose levels—a performance drop that was significantly more pronounced during moderate continuous exercise than during interval exercise. Based on these results, the paper recommends maintaining blood glucose within a more "prudent" range (above standard targets) during continuous exercise to account for overestimation and prevent unrecognized hypoglycemia, while highlighting the need for further research across additional types of physical activity.
