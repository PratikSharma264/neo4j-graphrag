The study “Is exercise a therapeutic tool for improvement of cardiovascular risk factors in adolescents with type 1 diabetes mellitus? A randomised controlled trial” is a therapeutic tool for improvement of cardiovascular risk factors in adolescents with type 1 diabetes mellitus? A randomised controlled trial evaluates the impact of a six-month exercise program on glycemic control, plasma lipid levels, blood pressure, hypoglycemia frequency, anthropometric measurements, and insulin requirements among 196 adolescents with type 1 diabetes mellitus. The findings reveal that regular exercise significantly improves glycemic control by reducing HbA1c  levels, decreases dyslipidemia (lowering total cholesterol, LDL-C, and triglycerides while increasing HDL-C), reduces daily insulin dosage requirements, and leads to beneficial reductions in body mass index (BMI) and waist circumference without increasing the frequency of hypoglycemic attacks. Based on these results, the paper concludes and recommends that exercise should be considered an indispensable therapeutic component in the medical management of adolescents with type 1 diabetes to optimize glycemic control and mitigate cardiovascular risk factors.
