The Strategies to Enhance New CGM Use in Early Childhood (SENCE) study evaluates the effects of continuous glucose monitoring combined with a family behavioral intervention (CGM+FBI) and CGM alone compared with standard blood glucose monitoring (BGM) over six months in children aged 2 to <8 years with type 1 diabetes. The findings demonstrate that while consistent CGM use over six months did not produce significant improvements in percent time in range (70–180 mg/dL), it significantly reduced mean time spent in hypoglycemia (<70 mg/dL) across both CGM groups compared to BGM, with the family behavioral intervention additionally providing clear benefits for parental well-being by reducing diabetes burden and fear of hypoglycemia.
