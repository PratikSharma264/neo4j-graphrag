“Efficacy and Safety of Statin Therapy in Children With Familial Hypercholesterolemia“ evaluates the 2-year efficacy and safety of pravastatin treatment (20 to 40 mg/day) compared to placebo in 214 children aged 8 to 18 years with heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia. The study found that 2 years of pravastatin therapy induced a statistically significant regression of mean carotid intima-media thickness (IMT) compared to progression in the placebo group (-0.010 mm vs. +0.005 mm), while significantly reducing low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) levels by 24.1%. Additionally, the findings demonstrated no adverse effects on growth, sexual maturation, hormone levels, or liver and muscle tissue enzymes, confirming that statin therapy is both effective in regressing early atherosclerosis and well-tolerated in pediatric patients with this condition.
