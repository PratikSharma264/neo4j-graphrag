"Laparoscopic Gastric Banding in Morbidly Obese Adolescents" evaluates whether laparoscopic adjustable silicone gastric banding (LAGB) is a safe and effective weight loss method for morbidly obese adolescents. In this prospective study of 17 patients with a median age of 17 years followed over a median of 25 months, body mass index fell from a median of 44.7 to 30.2 kg/m² at 24 months, with patients achieving a median excess weight loss of 59.3%, 76.5% losing at least 50% of their excess weight, and only two complications reported (one slipped band and one leaking port). The study concludes that LAGB is a safe and effective medium-term weight loss treatment for morbidly obese adolescents, though its role in preventing adult obesity and related diseases requires further long-term study.
