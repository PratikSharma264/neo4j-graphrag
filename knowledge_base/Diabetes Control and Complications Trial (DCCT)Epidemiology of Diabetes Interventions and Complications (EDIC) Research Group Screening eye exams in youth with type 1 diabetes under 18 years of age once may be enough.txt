The “Diabetes Control and Complications Trial (DCCT)/Epidemiology of Diabetes Interventions and Complications (EDIC) Research Group Screening eye exams in youth with type 1 diabetes under 18 years of age: once may be enough?”  evaluates the frequency and applicability of evidence-based screening strategies for diabetic retinopathy in patients with type 1 diabetes. In response to concerns regarding the generalizability of their original findings, the authors note that historical clinical trial cohorts remain relevant, address changes in retinal photography methods (such as shifting from seven-field to one- or two-field screening), and emphasize that data-driven, personalized screening intervals offer an effective and cost-saving alternative to universal annual exams without compromising patient care.
