"Laparoscopic gastric plication in morbidly obese adolescents: a prospective study" evaluates the safety and efficacy of laparoscopic gastric plication (LGP) as a weight loss procedure in adolescents aged 11 to 17 years with morbid obesity. The study found that LGP achieved significant weight loss over two years—resulting in a mean percentage of excess weight loss (%EWL) of 68.2% and percentage of excess body mass index loss (%EBMIL) of 79.0%—alongside the resolution or improvement of all preoperative co-morbidities, with no deaths, minimal complications, and no rehospitalizations. Based on these findings, the paper concludes that LGP shows potential as an ideal conservative weight loss surgery for adolescents due to its effectiveness, minimal psychological disruption, and low risk of bleeding, leakage, or nutritional deficiency, though it recommends larger, well-designed studies with long-term follow-up to establish definitive clinical guidelines.
