"Improved transition to adult care in youth with type 1 diabetes: a pragmatic clinical trial" evaluates the effect of a communication technology-enhanced transition coordinator intervention compared with usual care on clinic attendance among youth aged 17–18 years transitioning from paediatric to adult diabetes care. The study found that the intervention group experienced a significant fourfold reduction in loss to follow-up, with only 11.9% failing to attend any outpatient diabetes appointments within the first year post-transfer compared to 47.1% in the usual care group, along with a lower proportion of individuals maintaining an abnormally high mean HbA1c (>9%) post-transition. While overall glycaemic control, emergency department visits, and diabetic ketoacidosis rates showed no significant differences between the groups, the programme successfully improved clinic attendance using simple, readily accessible communication technologies such as text messaging and emails.
