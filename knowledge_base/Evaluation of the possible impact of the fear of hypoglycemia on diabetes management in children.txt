The study Evaluation of the possible impact of the fear of hypoglycemia on diabetes management in children and adolescents with type 1 diabetes mellitus and their parents: a cross-sectional study evaluates the association between self-reported fear of hypoglycemia (FoH) in 100 Greek children and adolescents with type 1 diabetes mellitus (T1DM) and their parents using the Hypoglycemia Fear Survey (HFS), alongside demographic and clinical factors such as age, T1DM duration, recent HbA1c levels, treatment mode, and hypoglycemic event frequency. The findings demonstrate that parents experience significantly higher levels of FoH across all subscales compared to their children, while factors like age, diabetes duration, HbA1c, and treatment mode showed no overall significant correlation with HFS scores, though parents of children with HbA1c > 7.0% exhibited higher behavior and total fear scores. Based on these results, the paper recommends that healthcare providers routinely screen for parental FoH and focus on supportive intervention strategies to alleviate their psychological burden and optimize childhood diabetes management.
