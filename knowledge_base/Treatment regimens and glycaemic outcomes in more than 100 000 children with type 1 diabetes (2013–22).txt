The study Treatment regimens and glycaemic outcomes in more than 100 000 children with type 1 diabetes (2013–22): a longitudinal analysis of data from paediatric diabetes registries evaluates temporal trends in HbA1c levels, treatment modalities, and acute complications across international paediatric diabetes registries from 2013 to 2022. Between 2013 and 2022, aggregate mean HbA1c dropped from 8.2% to 7.6%, and the proportion of children reaching an HbA1c target below 7% increased from 19.0% to 38.8%, alongside marked increases in insulin pump usage (42.9% to 60.2%) and continuous glucose monitoring uptake (18.7% in 2016 to 81.7% in 2022). Concurrently, event rates per 100 person-years declined for both severe hypoglycaemia (from 3.0 to 1.7) and diabetic ketoacidosis (from 3.1 to 2.2). Despite these improvements coinciding with increased adoption of diabetes technology, many children still fail to achieve current International Society for Pediatric and Adolescent Diabetes (ISPAD) glycaemic targets, indicating that further advances in management are required even for youth with access to specialized care and technology.
