“Long-Term Complications in Youth-Onset Type 2 Diabetes Reply” evaluates the potential benefits and risks of bariatric surgery compared to standard medical therapy in young individuals with obesity and type 2 diabetes. Findings from harmonized analyses in the Teen-LABS and TODAY studies demonstrate that bariatric surgery is associated with lower odds of diabetic kidney disease (DKD) and cardiovascular disease compared with standard medical therapy. However, the authors emphasize several potential risks associated with weight loss surgery, including the need for repeat surgery, lifelong nutrient supplementation to manage dietary deficiencies, adverse impacts on bone health, potential effects on offspring, and an increasing mental health burden, along with potential access barriers due to unequal insurance coverage in the United States. Additionally, the paper highlights seminal findings regarding the determinants of DKD in youth- and adult-onset type 2 diabetes from studies such as the Determinants of Diabetic Nephropathy and the Diabetic Renal Disease Study in Pima people, noting that these insights will be published in the future.
