The paper, "Contemporary Roles of the Pediatric Psychologist in Diabetes Care," evaluates the diverse clinical, administrative, and research roles that pediatric psychologists play in managing diabetes among children and adolescents across various treatment phases—including diagnosis, outpatient visits, and inpatient hospitalizations—and settings such as academic medical centers, private practice, and community organizations. Findings highlight that major healthcare organizations recognize psychologists as essential members of the interdisciplinary diabetes care team, contributing through screening, individual, family, and group interventions, as well as translational research and program development for youth transitioning to adult care. The authors recommend embedding pediatric psychologists directly into integrated diabetes clinics to deliver comprehensive medical and psychosocial care in a single visit, alongside increasing advocacy to expand clinical engagement, billing models, and translational research opportunities in pediatric diabetes management.
