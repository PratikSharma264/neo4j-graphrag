Prevalence of Overweight and Obesity in Youth with Diabetes in USA: The SEARCH for Diabetes in Youth Study examined the prevalence of overweight and obesity among youth with type 1 and type 2 diabetes and compared them with youth without diabetes. Among youth with type 2 diabetes, 79.4% were obese and 10.4% were overweight, while among those with type 1 diabetes, 12.6% were obese and 22.1% were overweight. Youth with type 1 diabetes had a higher prevalence of overweight than youth without diabetes (22.1% vs. 16.1%), although their overall obesity rate was not significantly higher. The findings highlight that obesity is particularly common among youth with type 2 diabetes, while overweight is also an important concern among youth with type 1 diabetes.
