The study "Mental health comorbidity and youth onset type 2 diabetes: A systematic review of the literature" evaluates the current literature surrounding youth with depression and type 2 diabetes (T2D), specifically reviewing 13 publications from 8 studies involving 2,244 individuals aged 6 to 17. The findings indicate that while depressive symptoms are common in youth with T2D and correlate with negative T2D outcomes, most studies failed to formally assess for major depressive disorder (MDD) and actively excluded participants with a prior MDD diagnosis. Based on these gaps, the authors recommend that future research focus on four key areas: including youth with diagnosed MDD, conducting treatment studies addressing both T2D and MDD concurrently, analyzing larger and more racially diverse youth samples, and evaluating how social determinants of health alongside mental health comorbidities impact T2D outcomes.
