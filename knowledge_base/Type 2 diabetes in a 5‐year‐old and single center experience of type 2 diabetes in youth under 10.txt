“Type 2 diabetes in a 5‐year‐old and single center experience of type 2 diabetes in youth under 10" evaluates the clinical presentation, diagnosis, and treatment of early-onset type 2 diabetes mellitus in children under 10 years old by discussing a specific case study of a 5-year-old Hispanic male and presenting a retrospective review of 20 pediatric patients under age 10 at a single diabetes center. The study finds that while type 2 diabetes remains uncommon in prepubertal children, it merits careful consideration and diagnostic evaluation—particularly among high-risk populations such as Hispanic youth presenting with abnormal weight gain, acanthosis nigricans, elevated HbA1c, and negative type 1 autoantibody testing.
