"Use of Continuous Glucose Monitoring in Youth-Onset Type 2 Diabetes" evaluates the emerging roles, applications, and current research landscape of continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) technology in youth-onset type 2 diabetes. The review finds that CGM provides critical insights into free-living glycemic patterns, glucose variability, and cardiometabolic risk markers in obese and at-risk youth, revealing key discrepancies when compared to traditional screening tests like HbA1c and oral glucose tolerance tests. However, it highlights that interventional evidence regarding CGM's effectiveness in directly improving long-term glycemic control specifically in youth with type 2 diabetes remains very limited. Consequently, the paper recommends conducting rigorous future studies to address these knowledge gaps and establish dedicated guidelines for utilizing advanced glucose monitoring tools to optimize clinical outcomes in this young population facing an aggressive form of the disease.
