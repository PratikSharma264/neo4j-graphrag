The Type 1 Diabetes Exercise Initiative Pediatric (T1DEXIP) study evaluates glucose changes during physical activity and identifies factors influencing these changes in adolescents with type 1 diabetes. Analyzing data from 251 participants who logged 3,738 activities, the study found that average blood glucose levels decreased from 163 ± 66 mg/dL pre-activity to 148 ± 66 mg/dL by the end of activity. Greater glucose drops were associated with participant-level factors such as lower baseline HbA1c, shorter diabetes duration, lower BMI, and reduced hypoglycemia fear, as well as event-level factors including noncompetitive activities, insulin on board > 0.05 units/kg, pre-activity glucose dropping or above 150 mg/dL, and high time in range prior to exercise. The authors conclude that recognizing these participant- and event-level predictors can help improve decision support tools and self-management strategies to minimize activity-induced dysglycemia in youth with type 1 diabetes
