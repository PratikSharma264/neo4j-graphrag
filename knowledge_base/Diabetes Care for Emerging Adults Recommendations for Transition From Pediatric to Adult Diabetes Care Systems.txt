"Diabetes Care for Emerging Adults: Recommendations for Transition From Pediatric to Adult Diabetes Care Systems" evaluates the critical gaps, challenges, and systemic differences involved when youth with type 1 and type 2 diabetes transition from pediatric to adult health care systems, finding that this period is characterized by a high risk of disengagement from care, poor glycemic control, acute and chronic complications, and psychosocial burdens like depression and eating disorders, and consequently recommends establishing structured transitional frameworks, improving continuity and multidisciplinary support, addressing insurance gaps under healthcare reforms, and fostering patient self-management readiness tailored to the developmental stages of emerging adulthood.
