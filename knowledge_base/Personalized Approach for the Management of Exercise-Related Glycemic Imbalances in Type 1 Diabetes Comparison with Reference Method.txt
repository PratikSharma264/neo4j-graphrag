Personalized Approach for the Management of Exercise-Related Glycemic Imbalances in Type 1 Diabetes: Comparison with Reference Method evaluates the efficacy of the personalized Exercise Carbohydrate Requirement Estimation System (ECRES) algorithm against a reference tabular approach to estimate extra carbohydrate requirements for preventing exercise-induced hypoglycemia in 26 adults with type 1 diabetes. The study found that the ECRES algorithm calculated a significantly lower median carbohydrate intake overall compared to the tabular approach (13.5 g vs. 23.0 g; P < .01), which was particularly marked in physically active participants (9.0 g vs. 23.0 g; P < .01), while both methods successfully prevented hypoglycemia during exercise; post-exercise, ECRES maintained continuous glucose monitoring metrics within recommended target guidelines, whereas the tabular method resulted in significantly higher time below range in the afternoon (11.8% vs. target <4%; P < .05) and time above range during the night (39.3% vs. target <25%; P < .05).
