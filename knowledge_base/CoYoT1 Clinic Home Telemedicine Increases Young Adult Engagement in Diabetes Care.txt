"CoYoT1 Clinic: Home Telemedicine Increases Young Adult Engagement in Diabetes Care" evaluates whether an innovative shared telemedicine appointment care model (CoYoT1 Clinic) for young adults aged 18 to 25 with type 1 diabetes improves care engagement, patient satisfaction, and adherence to American Diabetes Association (ADA) guidelines regarding appointment frequency compared to a control group receiving treatment as usual. The study found that CoYoT1 patients attended significantly more clinic visits, increased their visit frequency from the preceding year, utilized diabetes technologies more frequently, and reported greater satisfaction with care. Notably, 74% of patients in the CoYoT1 group met the ADA guideline of being seen four times over the 12-month study period, whereas none of the control group members met this recommendation. Based on these findings, the paper concludes that home telemedicine effectively increases young adults' adherence to ADA guidelines, enhances the usage of diabetes technologies, and improves retention in care during the challenging transition period into adulthood.
