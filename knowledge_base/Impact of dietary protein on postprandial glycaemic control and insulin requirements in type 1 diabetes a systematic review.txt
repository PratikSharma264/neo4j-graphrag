The study "Impact of dietary protein on postprandial glycaemic control and insulin requirements in type 1 diabetes: a systematic review" evaluates the glycemic impact of dietary protein and the corresponding insulin requirements for individuals living with Type 1 diabetes. By synthesizing evidence from a systematic literature search across biomedical databases, the review highlights that dietary protein significantly contributes to postprandial glycemic excursions alongside carbohydrates, leading to delayed hyperglycemia. Based on these findings, the paper discusses recommendations and clinical guidelines to consider dietary protein composition—not just carbohydrate content alone—when calculating mealtime insulin doses to improve overall postprandial glycemic control.
