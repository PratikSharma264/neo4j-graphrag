"Transition to adult diabetes care in Germany—High risk for acute complications and declining metabolic control during the transition phase" evaluates the impact of transferring young adults with type 1 diabetes from pediatric to adult health care using routine data from 1283 patients retrieved via the German/Austrian DPV database. The study's findings reveal that during the first year after transition, metabolic control significantly worsened as HbA1c increased from 8.95% to 9.20%, while the frequency of diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) with hospitalization and severe hypoglycemia both doubled. Furthermore, microvascular complications increased dramatically depending on the time interval between the first adult visit and the last pediatric visit. Based on these findings, the paper concludes that structured transition programs incorporating case management are needed to improve the transition process and overall health outcomes.
