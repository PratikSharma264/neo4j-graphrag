"Components of Interventions That Improve Transitions to Adult Care for Adolescents With Type 1 Diabetes" evaluates the effectiveness of various transition program components in improving health outcomes for adolescents and young adults with type 1 diabetes transferring from pediatric to adult care through a systematic review and meta-analysis of 18 studies involving 3,382 participants. The findings indicate that while most studies reported improvement or maintenance of hemoglobin A1c levels post-transition, pooling data from controlled studies showed no significant differences in HbA1c at 12 months, though transition programs demonstrated consistent success in reducing episodes of diabetic ketoacidosis. The paper notes that further research is needed to determine the most effective program types, and guidelines or explicit recommendations regarding specific program protocols were not detailed in the provided text.
