The study Physical Activity and Nutrition Interventions for Type 1 Diabetes: A Meta-analysis evaluates the effects of physical activity, nutrition-based, and combined hybrid interventions on glycemic control (measured via HbA1c levels) and health-related quality of life among children and adolescents aged 18 and below with type 1 diabetes mellitus (T1DM). Analyzing 18 randomized controlled trials, the study found that neither physical activity alone nor nutrition-based interventions alone produced statistically significant reductions in HbA1c levels compared to control groups, though results generally trended favorably toward enhancing glycemic control and quality of life, with initial evidence from a single hybrid study showing promise for multi-component interventions. Based on these findings, the study recommend further research with larger, high-quality trials to establish concrete guidelines and better understand the long-term potential of structured lifestyle and combined hybrid interventions for pediatric T1DM management.
