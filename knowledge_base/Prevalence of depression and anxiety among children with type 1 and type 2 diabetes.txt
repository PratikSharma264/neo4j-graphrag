Prevalence of depression and anxiety among children with type 1 and type 2 diabetes: a systematic review and meta-analysis evaluates the prevalence of depression and anxiety among children with type 1 and type 2 diabetes by analyzing 109 studies involving 52,493 pediatric patients across Web of Science, Scopus, and PubMed databases between 2000 and 2020. The review found a 22.2% prevalence of depression and a 17.7% prevalence of anxiety among children with type 1 diabetes, with depression being notably higher in girls (29.7%) compared to boys (19.7%) and in lower-middle-income countries (29.3%), while the prevalence of depression among children with type 2 diabetes stood at 22.7%. Based on these findings, the study highlights the necessity of implementing extensive, periodic psychological screening and targeted interventions to identify and reduce pediatric depression in this vulnerable population.
