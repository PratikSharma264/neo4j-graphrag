"Emerging adulthood: A theory of development from the late teens through the twenties." evaluates a theoretical conception of human development focused specifically on ages 18 through 25, proposing it as a distinct period separate from adolescence and young adulthood. The paper presents theoretical background and supporting evidence indicating that this phase is unique demographically, subjectively, and through intensive identity explorations, while also outlining a cultural context demonstrating that this period exists exclusively in societies allowing young people a prolonged phase of independent role exploration.
