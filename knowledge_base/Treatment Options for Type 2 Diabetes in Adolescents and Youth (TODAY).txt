The "Treatment Options for Type 2 Diabetes in Adolescents and Youth (TODAY)" study evaluates the implementation and efficacy of a standard diabetes education (SDE) program delivered to youth aged 10 to 18 with type 2 diabetes and their family support persons. The paper finds that 79% of families achieved full mastery of the SDE curriculum in an average of 5.5±1.3 sessions, with 62% mastering individual session topics in a single visit, though participants with a longer duration of diabetes or those requiring a translator needed significantly more sessions to reach mastery. While specific clinical recommendations and formal guidelines were not established in this feasibility report, the authors emphasize that initial diabetes education at diagnosis is insufficient on its own for long-term mastery, highlighting the need for continuous, tailored educational programs to ensure young patients and their families develop essential diabetes self-management skills.
