The study Youth Involvement in the Decision to Start CGM Predicts Subsequent CGM Use evaluates whether youth involvement in the decision to initiate continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) influences subsequent CGM adherence, and tests whether CGM self-efficacy and perceived hassle mediate this relationship among parent-youth dyads. The findings reveal that youth involvement in the initial decision positively predicted CGM use between weeks 5 and 12, mediated by the youth perceiving higher self-efficacy and lower hassle at a 2-month follow-up. Based on these findings, the authors recommend that healthcare providers actively facilitate youth participation and input when deciding to initiate CGM therapy in order to maximize long-term device adherence and clinical benefits.
