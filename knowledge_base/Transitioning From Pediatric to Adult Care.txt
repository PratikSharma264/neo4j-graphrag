"Transitioning From Pediatric to Adult Care" evaluates the unique challenges faced by emerging adults (ages ~18–30) with type 1 diabetes as they move from pediatric to adult health care systems. The paper highlights that this post-high school period involves navigating complex personal and developmental transitions, during which many young adults experience increased risk for poor glycemic control, acute metabolic complications, and early microvascular damage—particularly among high-risk subgroups such as young women dealing with disordered eating. Findings indicate that traditional transition models often result in severe gaps in medical care or patients being lost to follow-up entirely. To address these issues, the authors recommend that both pediatric and adult providers adopt a developmentally sensitive, nonjudgmental framework, integrate patients' individual life circumstances into care plans, and ensure continuous, coordinated medical support.
