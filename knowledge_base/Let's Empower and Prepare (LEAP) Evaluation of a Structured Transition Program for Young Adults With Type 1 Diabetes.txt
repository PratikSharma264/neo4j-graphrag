"Let's Empower and Prepare (LEAP): Evaluation of a Structured Transition Program for Young Adults With Type 1 Diabetes" evaluates the efficacy of a structured transition program compared to usual care in improving routine clinical follow-up, glycemic control, and psychosocial outcomes among young adults transitioning from pediatric to adult diabetes care. The study found that while limitations prevented direct comparisons of adult care visits with the control group, 78% of the intervention group participants discharged from pediatric care successfully established adult care visits, and the intervention group demonstrated significantly improved glycemic control, a lower incidence of severe hypoglycemia, and better global well-being compared to the control group.
