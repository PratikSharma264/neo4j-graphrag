"Clinical Practice Guideline for the Evaluation and Treatment of Children and Adolescents With Obesity" evaluates evidence-based evaluation and treatment strategies for children and adolescents with overweight and obesity to inform pediatric healthcare providers on standard, longitudinal, and patient-centered care. The publication highlights findings that obesity is a common, complex, and persistent chronic disease affecting millions of youth, driven by a combination of genetic, environmental, socioecological, and structural factors. Key recommendations and guidelines established in the paper include measuring height and weight to assess BMI percentiles at least annually for children aged 2 to 18 years, as well as evaluating for comorbidities such as dyslipidemia, prediabetes/diabetes, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease, and hypertension. Management guidelines recommend treating obesity and comorbidities concurrently within a medical home framework using motivational interviewing, referring patients aged 6 and older to intensive health behavior and lifestyle treatment involving at least 26 face-to-face contact hours over 3 to 12 months, offering weight loss pharmacotherapy as an adjunct for adolescents aged 12 and older with obesity, and offering referrals for metabolic and bariatric surgery evaluation for adolescents aged 13 and older with severe obesity.
