"Closing the Gap: Results of the Multicenter Canadian Randomized Controlled Trial of Structured Transition in Young Adults With Type 1 Diabetes" evaluates the effectiveness of an 18-month structured transition program featuring a transition coordinator on clinic attendance, glycemic control, satisfaction with care, and diabetes-related distress among young adults aged 17 to 20 with type 1 diabetes. The study found that during the intervention period, participants in the transition program had improved clinic attendance, greater satisfaction with care, and decreased diabetes-related distress compared to those receiving standard care, alongside a trend toward lower HbA1c levels, though these benefits were not sustained 12 months after the intervention ended.
